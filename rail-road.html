Rail & Road Logistics

Integrated freight and transport solutions for industries operating across South Africa and Zimbabwe. Reliable scheduling, bulk commodity handling, and cross-border capability.

What We Do

Moving Freight Across Southern Africa

Rail and road logistics is the core of Kudenga Group's operations. We provide end-to-end freight management across the Southern African region with a focus on reliability, safety, and cost efficiency.

Our logistics capability covers bulk commodity freight, scheduled transport runs, and cross-border corridor management between South Africa and Zimbabwe. We work with mining houses, agricultural producers, manufacturers, and distributors requiring dependable inbound and outbound logistics.

Where clients require a combination of rail and road transport, our team coordinates multi-modal solutions that optimise cost and transit time for each specific route and commodity type.

Our Services

Rail & Road Service Offering

Our logistics services are structured around what the Southern African freight market actually demands — flexibility, cross-border capability, and sector-specific knowledge.

Bulk Commodity Rail Freight

Scheduled rail freight services for bulk commodities including minerals, agricultural products, and manufactured goods. Coordinated through established rail corridors across South Africa and Zimbabwe.

Road Freight & Transport

Road-based freight services for consignments that require flexible scheduling or direct delivery to destinations beyond the rail network. Suitable for time-sensitive or smaller-volume loads.

Cross-Border Corridor Management

End-to-end logistics management for freight crossing the South Africa–Zimbabwe border. Includes customs coordination, transit documentation, and handover between rail and road modes.

Multi-Modal Logistics Solutions

Where a single transport mode is insufficient, we coordinate rail, road, and intermodal transfers to deliver an integrated solution optimised for the client's route and commodity.

Freight Scheduling & Route Planning

Logistics planning support for clients requiring structured inbound or outbound freight scheduling. We work with clients' operational teams to build reliable supply chain routines.

Mining Logistics Support

Specialist freight coordination for mining clients requiring movement of ore, equipment, and consumables. Aligned with our Mining Division for clients who require both extraction and logistics support.
