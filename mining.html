Mining Division

Mining & Consulting

Operational mining services and specialist consulting for the extraction and resources sector. Active across South Africa and Zimbabwe.

Our Mining Capability

Resources Expertise Across Southern Africa

Kudenga Group's Mining Division provides operational support and consulting services to mining operations across the Southern African region. Our team combines practical on-site experience with sector-specific knowledge of the regulatory and operational environment.

We work with junior and established miners, providing consulting on operational efficiency, logistics integration, safety compliance, and project planning. Where required, we also provide direct operational support through embedded team deployments on active mine sites.

Our Mining Division works closely with the Group's Rail and Road Logistics division to provide clients with end-to-end solutions from extraction through to delivery.

Service Offering

Mining Services & Consulting

Mining Operations Consulting

Specialist consulting for operational efficiency, production planning, and resource optimisation on active mine sites. Delivered by experienced practitioners with direct Southern African mining exposure.

Safety & Compliance Advisory

Support for mining operations seeking to align with South African and Zimbabwean mining safety legislation and regulatory requirements. Includes audit support, procedure development, and staff training guidance.

Logistics Integration for Mining

Coordination of inbound supply and outbound ore logistics for mining clients. Delivered in partnership with Kudenga Group's Rail and Road Logistics division for end-to-end supply chain management.

Project Planning & Feasibility

Operational planning support for new mine development, expansion projects, or operational restructuring. Covers logistics, manpower, equipment, and regulatory requirements.

On-Site Operational Support

Embedded operational support teams for mining clients requiring experienced personnel on site. Scope and duration agreed per engagement.

Equipment & Resource Procurement Advisory

Guidance on equipment selection, procurement, and supplier engagement for mining operations in the Southern African context. To be confirmed — details to be supplied by client.
