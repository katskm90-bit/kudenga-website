About Kudenga Group

Built on the Ground.
Proven Across Borders.

Kudenga Group is a Southern African holding company with deep operational roots in rail and road logistics, mining, and real estate. With a footprint spanning South Africa and Zimbabwe, we bring practical expertise and local knowledge to every project we undertake.

  • Over two decades of operational experience across Southern Africa
  • Multi-sector capacity across logistics, mining, and hospitality
  • Active operations and partner networks in South Africa and Zimbabwe
  • Committed to long-term, accountable partnerships with clients
